## athenahealth unveils AI-powered patient communication tools
URL: https://www.bizbot.com/news/athenahealth-ai-powered-patient-communication-tools/
Date: February 19, 2026

**BOSTON, February 19, 2026** – athenahealth, a leading provider of network-enabled healthcare services, has announced the introduction of its next-generation AI-native patient engagement tools within its flagship athenaOne® platform. These advanced capabilities aim to transform how patients and healthcare practices interact, offering more efficient communication and streamlined administrative workflows.

The updated suite introduces innovative text and voice communication tools designed to simplify patient-provider interactions. Patients will now have 24/7 access to virtual assistants that can handle routine inquiries and appointment scheduling, all seamlessly integrated into athenaOne workflows. These enhancements aim to improve the patient experience while reducing the administrative burden on healthcare staff.

"Patient–provider communication still operates much like it did decades ago, and that model can't scale in today's environment as practices get busier", said Paul Brient, chief product and operations officer at athenahealth. "Our AI assistants can handle these routine interactions reliably, reducing unnecessary work and making the system more dependable for both patients and care teams."

### Key Features of the New Tools

#### Patient Conversations

The centerpiece of the update is a conversational AI feature offering both text and voice functionalities. The text feature, currently in alpha testing and slated for wider availability in the first half of this year, uses real-time two-way texting and secure web chat to address common patient queries. Patients can seamlessly transition to human staff for further assistance if needed, retaining the context of their interaction. The voice feature, expected in the second half of the year, will act as a virtual front desk assistant, providing multilingual support for inbound calls and handling routine requests without delays. Future enhancements will include real-time language translation and intelligent task automation to maximize efficiency.

"These new text and voice conversation capabilities will transform how we connect with patients by providing instant responses that speed issue resolution and save valuable time", said Lesley Terry, practice manager at Red River Family Practice in Austin, Texas. "For our practice, these tools will reduce phone call volume and backlog by allowing us to handle conversations promptly without putting patients on hold. Overall, this is transformative for both patient convenience and practice workflow."

#### Waitlist Scheduling

A new waitlist scheduling feature, available in the first half of this year, will use AI to identify open appointment slots due to cancellations. Patients will be notified via text message to fill these gaps, enhancing access to care and ensuring more efficient use of clinician schedules.

#### Intelligent Patient Assistant

Set to launch in the second half of this year, the intelligent assistant will be integrated into athenahealth's patient portal and athenaPatient mobile app. This feature will help patients reschedule appointments, check payment balances, and better understand their medical records, reducing common inquiries to practice staff.

#### Enhanced Patient Self-Scheduling

Also expected later this year, the self-scheduling feature will allow patients to book appointments by describing their needs in plain language. The system will analyze this input to match patients with the appropriate provider, appointment type, and time slot, improving scheduling accuracy and convenience.

### A Broader Vision for AI in Healthcare

These updates reflect athenahealth's continued investment in embedding artificial intelligence across its suite of clinical, patient engagement, and administrative solutions. By prioritizing AI-driven innovations, the company aims to make healthcare more accessible, efficient, and patient-centered.

For nearly three decades, athenahealth has been a leader in advancing healthcare technology. The company connects providers, payers, and patients, creating an ecosystem designed to deliver high-quality, sustainable care. As part of its ongoing mission, athenahealth's latest tools underscore the potential for AI to modernize and enhance the healthcare experience for millions of Americans.

## OpenAI Introduces Pulse: ChatGPT's New Personalization Feature
URL: https://www.bizbot.com/news/openai-pulse-chatgpt-personalization/
Date: 28 September 2025

OpenAI has launched Pulse, a personalized daily update feature for ChatGPT Pro subscribers priced at $200 monthly. The tool analyzes user activity overnight to deliver customized insights aimed at improving productivity.

### How Pulse Functions

Available exclusively to ChatGPT Pro users, Pulse leverages "chat history, saved memory, and user feedback to generate customized updates." The system can integrate with external services including Gmail and Google Calendar, providing reminders about meetings, gift suggestions, and recipe ideas displayed as interactive visual cards.

Users maintain control over Pulse's access and data usage, with OpenAI emphasizing privacy as a priority. The updates are temporary—available only on their delivery day—though users may save them within chats for future reference.

### Rollout and Pricing

Currently limited to ChatGPT Pro ($200/month), OpenAI plans to expand Pulse to Plus subscribers ($20/month) and eventually all users. The company acknowledges the feature occasionally generates irrelevant suggestions, such as updates on already-completed tasks.

### Market Competition

Pulse enters a competitive landscape alongside Samsung's Now Bar, Perplexity's Comet browser, and similar AI-assisted tools. OpenAI positions Pulse as transforming ChatGPT "from a reactive chatbot" into a proactive digital assistant designed for seamless daily integration.

## AI Startup Cohere Secures $6.8B Valuation in Latest Funding
URL: https://www.bizbot.com/news/ai-startup-cohere-secures-68b-valuation-in-latest-funding/
Date: 15 September 2025

Canadian AI startup Cohere has achieved a $6.8 billion valuation following a $500 million funding round. The company specializes in enterprise-focused artificial intelligence solutions and aims to strengthen its competitive position.

Radical Ventures and Inovia Capital led the funding, with support from AMD Ventures, Nvidia, PSP Investments, and Salesforce Ventures. Cohere distinguishes itself by creating customized AI models for businesses, contrasting with broader approaches from competitors like OpenAI and Meta.

Co-founder Nick Frosst stated: "The funding allows us to expand more globally, branch off into different modalities...and keep building secure AI" for enterprises.

### Leadership Expansion

Joelle Pineau, formerly Meta's VP of AI Research, joined as chief AI officer. She previously directed Meta's Fundamental AI Research group for eight years before departing in May.

François Chadwick, with prior experience at Uber and Shield AI, was appointed chief financial officer.

### Enterprise AI Development

Cohere launched North, a ChatGPT-style platform assisting knowledge workers with tasks like document summarization. The company plans to expand into agentic AI systems designed to help organizations operate more efficiently.

The funding reflects broader investment momentum in AI, with the company intending to explore additional technological areas and expand its global market presence.

## Stripe Expands Small Business Lending with New Australia Launch
URL: https://www.bizbot.com/news/stripe-expands-small-business-lending-with-new-australia-launch/
Date: 15 September 2025

Global payments company Stripe has introduced Stripe Capital in Australia, a rapid lending solution integrated with its payments platform. The announcement occurred during Stripe Tour Sydney, addressing a significant challenge: nearly half of Australian small business owners struggle to secure funding amid rising costs.

Stripe reported reaching one million users across Australia and New Zealand, with tens of thousands of new businesses joining monthly.

### Faster Lending for Small Businesses

Stripe Capital streamlines the lending process by using existing payment data to pre-approve funding without extensive paperwork. Approved funds arrive within one to two business days, with repayments automatically adjusted to sales performance. The service features transparent flat fees with no penalties for early repayment or late payments.

Karl Durrance, Stripe's managing director for Australia and New Zealand, stated: "SMBs are the backbone of the Australian economy, but the cost of doing business has risen sharply and around half report difficulty securing funding."

Platforms built on Stripe, including hospitality app me&u, can now offer Stripe Capital to their customers.

### Economic Context

Stripe's research indicates 92% of Australian business leaders anticipate challenging conditions ahead, citing rising costs (51%), economic uncertainty (38%), and customer loyalty struggles (28%).

### Technology Adoption Trends

Despite economic concerns, 85% of business leaders feel confident adopting new tools. Additionally, 70% leverage AI for customer service (39%), fraud detection (32%), and sales/marketing (31%). Over half of respondents (53%) use or plan to use stablecoins for cross-border transactions.

### Additional Product Announcements

Stripe unveiled over 50 product upgrades, including the Stripe Reader S710 (cellular-enabled card reader), Stripe Orchestration (multi-processor management), and Stripe Startups (early-stage company support program).

## Google Releases Free AI-Powered Video Editing Tools for Public Use
URL: https://www.bizbot.com/news/google-releases-free-ai-powered-video-editing-tools-for-public-use/
Date: 29 August 2025

Google has launched Google Videos, an AI-powered video editing platform integrated into Google Workspace, designed to simplify video creation for professional and casual users alike.

### Simplified Video Editing with AI

The platform offers several innovative features. Users can "generate videos using AI avatars" by uploading scripts and selecting from various voices and personas. This capability is now available to Workspace Business or Enterprise Starter users, Google AI Pro or Ultra subscribers, and Workspace for Education customers.

An automated editing tool removes filler words like "um" and "ah" while trimming extended pauses, helping creators produce polished content with minimal effort.

### New Capabilities and Upcoming Improvements

Google Videos now supports image-to-video generation, converting still images into eight-second clips. Future enhancements include noise cancellation, background effects comparable to Google Meet features, and flexible video sizing options (portrait, landscape, and square formats), though no timeline has been specified.

### Free Version for General Users

A free consumer version provides basic editing tools along with access to "Google's collection of templates, fonts, and stock media," making straightforward video editing accessible without premium features.

### Market Position

By embedding video tools directly into Workspace, Google differentiates itself from competitors like Synthesia and D-ID, offering budget-conscious users an integrated solution for video content creation.

## WhatsApp Unveils AI-Driven Writing Assistant with Privacy Features
URL: https://www.bizbot.com/news/whatsapp-unveils-ai-driven-writing-assistant-with-privacy-features/
Date: 29 August 2025

WhatsApp has launched "Writing Help," an AI-powered feature enabling users to enhance message clarity and professionalism directly within the app. The tool allows users to access rewriting styles including "professional, humorous, supportive, or error correction" by tapping a pencil icon and selecting preferences.

**Key functionality:** Recipients cannot detect if messages were revised using this feature. The tool generates multiple suggestions, letting users choose between the original text or AI alternatives.

**Privacy approach:** Meta emphasizes "Private Processing" technology, which encrypts requests sent anonymously to Meta's servers. According to the company, "neither WhatsApp, Meta, nor any third party can access or store the content of the messages." Original messages and suggestions remain unlinked to specific users and aren't retained.

**Availability:** Currently available in English for Android and iOS users in the United States and select countries. The feature requires users to enable "Private Processing" in settings first. Additional language support and regional expansion are planned for later in 2025, though no Hebrew or Israeli release date has been confirmed.

**Market context:** This launch positions Meta alongside competitors like Telegram, Microsoft, and Google in the AI-enhanced messaging space, combining writing assistance with privacy protections.
